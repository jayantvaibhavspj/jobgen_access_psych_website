# Access Psych assistant approved information

This file is the assistant's approved, factual reference. Keep it aligned with the published Access Psych pages. The local chat route only returns these approved sections; it does not generate clinical guidance.

## Booking
Appointments can be booked online through the Access Psych Treatment Booking Portal: https://clientportal.powerdiary.com/clientportal/accesspsych

To make a booking or referral request, visit /online-booking-request. For a friendly confidential chat, call 1800 APSYCH (1800 277 924) or email info@accesspsych.com.au.

## Clinicians
Access Psych has a team of mental health clinicians located across Australia. View clinician profiles and find a clinician near you at /meet-our-team. Access Psych offers telehealth and face-to-face sessions.

## Fees and Medicare
Access Psych is not a bulk billing clinic, so payment is required on the day before your counselling session. If your therapy is covered by Medicare, Access Psych will assist with processing all eligible rebates. For details, visit /fees or call 1800 APSYCH (1800 277 924).

## Employee Assistance Program
Access Psych provides tailored Employee Assistance Programs to businesses across Australia. EAP is a confidential and solution-focused counselling service for employees experiencing personal, family and/or work-related issues. Learn more at /employee-assistance-program.

## Locations
Access Psych operates a selection of clinics nationally for face-to-face appointments and offers counselling and psychology sessions via telehealth. View clinic details at /our-locations or call 1800 APSYCH (1800 277 924).

## Contact
Call 1800 APSYCH (1800 277 924) or email info@accesspsych.com.au. Booking and referral requests can be made at /online-booking-request.

## Frequently Asked Questions
Q: Is there an Access Psych mental health clinician near me?
A: Access Psych operates a selection of clinics nationally for face-to-face appointments and offers all counselling and psychology sessions via telehealth. View clinic details at /our-locations.
Q: How can I find out more about counselling and psychological services?
A: Visit the Access Psych website or contact the team on 1800 APSYCH (1800 277 924) or info@accesspsych.com.au.
Q: Do you offer relationship counselling for couples?
A: Yes. Access Psych offers therapy and counselling sessions for couples. Medicare rebates do not apply for these services.
Q: Can you help with anxiety and depression?
A: Access Psych clinicians support people experiencing anxiety and depression. Visit /psychology-therapy-counselling or contact the team to learn more.
Q: Do you have a waitlist for psychologist support?
A: Access Psych states that appointments can be provided immediately, with little or no waiting time. Contact 1800 APSYCH (1800 277 924) to make an appointment.
Q: Is it possible to complete my appointment via telehealth?
A: Yes. Access Psych offers therapy, counselling and psychology sessions via telehealth, as well as face-to-face sessions at selected clinics.
Q: Do you offer trauma psychology services?
A: Access Psych clinicians support clients living with a wide range of trauma. Visit /psychology-therapy-counselling or contact the team for more information.
Q: How do I get in touch with Access Psych?
A: Call 1800 APSYCH (1800 277 924), email info@accesspsych.com.au, or visit /online-booking-request.
Q: How do I get a Mental Health Care Plan?
A: A GP can provide a Mental Health Care Plan. For questions about accessing Access Psych services, call 1800 APSYCH (1800 277 924).
Q: What happens if I don’t want to see my doctor?
A: You may access psychological treatment as a private client without obtaining a Mental Health Care Plan from your GP. Contact Access Psych on 1800 APSYCH (1800 277 924) or info@accesspsych.com.au.
